If any of these fire, it's a down week

  • Easy-run HR over 160 at 9:15/mi or slower, two runs in a row
  • A niggle that changes your stride: stop, two days off
  • Long run cut short or 30 s/mi slow: don't advance it next week
  • HRV down 7+ days, body battery not recovering 3 nights, or a week under 7 h sleep
  • Reps drop on leg-day working sets, or climbing quality falls off

Lift rules for this block

  • Legs are stacked after Friday's easy run, never the day before the long run.
  • Upper push is short (20–25 min) and sits on Tuesday; climbing covers all pulling.
  • Leave 2 reps in the tank on every set until week 5. No PR attempts in this block.
  • Bands (hips, glutes, shoulders) are the one thing that never gets skipped.
